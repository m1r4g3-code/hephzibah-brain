# Upwork Niche Strategy

Emmanuel's active niche on Upwork and the rotation strategy. This document tracks current positioning, past niches tested, and the intelligence accumulated from each.

---

## Current Active Niche

**Primary:** AI Automation + Workflow Engineering (n8n, Claude API, Zapier-replacement)

**Target client:** SaaS founders, digital agencies, and operations-heavy businesses who have manual processes eating engineer or ops time.

**Positioning statement:** "I build AI workflows that replace the manual work your team is doing every day — using n8n, Claude API, and your existing stack. Not a product. A custom system."

**Why this niche:**
- Emmanuel has proven delivery (German medical clinic: 4 workflows, 4 days)
- High demand as businesses adopt AI but don't know how to implement
- Premium pricing supported (automation ROI is quantifiable)
- Low AI saturation (most freelancers offer ChatGPT wrappers, not real workflow architecture)
- Aligns with cold outreach niche (US medical/admin clients)

**Secondary niche:** Full-stack web (Next.js/React/TypeScript) for startups needing MVPs or dashboard builds. Lower prestige but reliable income while primary niche compounds.

## Niche Evaluation Criteria

Before entering a new niche, score it:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Demand | Are jobs being posted regularly? (10+/week minimum) |
| Budget quality | Can the typical client afford $50+/hr or $2k+ fixed? |
| AI saturation | Can AI do most of this without a human expert? |
| Competition | How many qualified freelancers are in this niche? |
| Proof | Do we have a relevant case study or demo? |
| Repeat potential | Can a single client become a $10k+/year relationship? |

## Positioning Angles by Niche

### AI Automation

"Most automation freelancers build Zapier chains. I build AI-native workflows that think, not just route."

Hook: The cost of manual processes is visible (hours). The cost of bad automation is invisible (errors, exceptions, maintenance debt).

### Full-Stack Web (Secondary)

"Most devs ship features. I ship systems — with the performance and architecture decisions that don't become problems 6 months later."

Hook: The technical debt from "just ship it" is always discovered after the first investor demo or scale event.
